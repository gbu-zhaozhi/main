        Zhao Lab works on the **design and optimization of intelligent materials and structures**.

        Rather than analysing a structure that has already been chosen, we ask the inverse question: given a target mechanical or multifunctional response, what geometry, material distribution, and external stimulus field will produce it? Answering this requires computational mechanics, optimization algorithms, fabrication, and measurement to be developed together.

        The three directions below are interconnected: the architected materials of (A) provide the design space, the coupled physics of (B) adds actuation and sensing, and the application studies of (C) test whether physically intelligent behaviour can be realized and controlled in practice.
