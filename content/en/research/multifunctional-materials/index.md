A structure becomes multifunctional when the same material architecture that carries load also performs another task: actuating, sensing, or transducing energy. We study this mainly through **magneto-mechanical coupling**, which has the practical advantage of being actuated remotely and wirelessly.

Our focus is on making these coupled responses **designable**. Rather than accepting the coupling a given material happens to have, we optimize the architecture so that the coupling itself — magnetic actuation, mechanical deformation, and in some designs the electrical signal — follows a prescribed behaviour.

## What we work on

- **Magneto-mechanical coupling.** Hard- and soft-magnetic materials whose deformation under an applied field is programmed through their microstructure and magnetization pattern.
- **Magneto-electro-mechanical responses.** Architected magnetic soft materials in which mechanical deformation and electrical output are co-designed, so that a single structure can act as actuator and sensor.
- **Programmable actuation.** Magnetization patterns and geometries designed so that one structure produces different motions under different applied fields.
- **Experimental characterization.** Custom magnetic actuation setups and electrical measurement rigs, so that predicted coupled responses are checked against measured ones.
