        Zhao Lab at the School of Advanced Engineering, Great Bay University, is recruiting **research assistants, Master’s students, Ph.D. students, and postdoctoral researchers**.

        ### Who we are looking for

        We welcome students and researchers with backgrounds in:

        - Mechanics
        - Mechanical Engineering
        - Civil Engineering
        - Robotics
        - Applied Physics
        - Computer Science
        - Aerospace Engineering

        ### What the work is like

        The group combines **computational and experimental research**. Members can take part in the complete research chain, from the initial idea to a validated device:

        1. **Theoretical modelling** — continuum and nonlinear mechanics, coupled multiphysics formulations.
        2. **Optimization algorithms** — inverse design and topology optimization, and their implementation.
        3. **Scientific programming** — MATLAB and Python, high-performance computing, and custom design platforms.
        4. **Advanced manufacturing and 3D printing** — additive manufacturing of designed architectures, including direct ink writing of magnetic soft materials.
        5. **Mechanical testing and experimental validation** — mechanical testing, magnetic actuation, and electrical measurement.

        ### What we value

        - **Independent thinking** — the ability to formulate a question, not only to execute a task.
        - **Hands-on capability** — comfort with building, fabricating, and measuring in the lab.
        - **Interdisciplinary collaboration** — working across mechanics, materials, manufacturing, and control.
        - **Research development** — aiming at high-quality journal publications, and building the foundation for doctoral study, further study abroad, an academic career, or engineering R&D.

        ### How to apply

        Interested applicants are invited to e-mail **zhaoz@gbu.edu.cn** with:

        - a curriculum vitae,
        - a brief statement of research interests,
        - and, where available, representative papers, code, or project reports.

        We are glad to discuss research ideas by e-mail before any formal application.

        *This page does not constitute an offer of funding or a specific position. Availability of positions and funding is subject to the university’s admissions and appointment procedures; please confirm current details by e-mail.*
