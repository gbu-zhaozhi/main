Zhao Lab was established in the **School of Advanced Engineering, Great Bay University**, with **Zhi Zhao** joining as **Assistant Professor** in September 2026.

The group works on the **design and optimization of intelligent materials and structures**, combining computational mechanics, topology optimization, architected materials, and coupled multiphysics with additive manufacturing and experimental validation.

Before joining Great Bay University, Zhi Zhao was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where he also completed his Ph.D. in Civil Engineering.
