Zhao Lab is recruiting **research assistants, Master’s students, Ph.D. students, and postdoctoral researchers**.

We welcome applicants with backgrounds in mechanics, mechanical engineering, civil engineering, robotics, applied physics, computer science, and aerospace engineering. The group combines computational and experimental research, so members can take part in the complete chain from theoretical modelling and optimization algorithms to 3D printing and mechanical testing.

Interested applicants may e-mail **zhaoz@gbu.edu.cn** with a CV and a short statement of research interests.

See the [Openings](/en/openings/) page for details.
