        ### Embedded Systems

        This course is designed for senior undergraduates and graduate students in intelligent materials and structures. Organized around the "sensing–computing–actuation" loop, it covers the fundamental principles and development methods of embedded systems, with hands-on projects drawn from the lab's research. Topics include:

        - **Embedded hardware fundamentals**: microcontroller architectures (STM32, ESP32, etc.), minimal system design, and common peripherals such as GPIO, timers, ADC/DAC, and PWM
        - **Embedded programming**: advanced C, interrupts and DMA, and serial communication protocols (UART/I2C/SPI/CAN)
        - **Real-time operating systems**: task scheduling, FreeRTOS fundamentals, and multitasking program design
        - **Sensing and actuation**: sensor signal acquisition and filtering, actuator driving, and closed-loop control
        - **Capstone project**: embedded system design and debugging for intelligent material and structure applications (e.g., integrated sensing–actuation devices)
