        **Chao Wang, Zhi Zhao, Ming Zhou, Ole Sigmund, and X. Shelly Zhang.** “A comprehensive review of educational articles on structural and multidisciplinary optimization.” *Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization*, Vol. 64, pp. 2827–2880, 2021.

        This review was completed during Zhi Zhao’s doctoral studies at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, together with Prof. X. Shelly Zhang’s group and collaborators. It systematically surveys educational articles on structural and multidisciplinary optimization and compiles openly available codes and teaching material for optimization courses.
